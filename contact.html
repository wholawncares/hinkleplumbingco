What are the office hours?

Office hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday. Readers are invited to call (618) 677-6556 during those hours to describe a problem and discuss scope factors. No after-hours service claim is made on this site.

What should be ready before calling?

The address, the age of the building where that detail is available, a description of the problem, and what has changed all help frame the first checks. Photos of visible pipe materials or fixtures can help the discussion, and district or subdivision details are noted where they apply.

No. This site has no contact form, no account signup, and no newsletter signup, and no email address is published here. Contact is by phone at (618) 677-6556 during office hours, and the office address is 1610 W Main St, Belleville, IL 62220.

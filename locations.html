Ask us now
Why does this site cover only five areas?

Belleville has no official neighborhood list, and only five areas carried enough district, plan, or plat evidence for their own pages: three historic districts and two subdivisions. Other names lacked boundaries, era evidence, or city-limits confirmation. Work elsewhere in Belleville is discussed by phone during office hours.

How is an address placed in a historic district?

District boundaries are approximate in the sources read, so an address is confirmed against the city's Historic Preservation Commission map and properties list. That check settles whether local review rules apply. Readers can call during office hours to discuss how a specific address is placed.

Are ZIP codes used as service boundaries?

No. ZIP codes are mailing areas, and every Belleville-associated ZIP is shared with neighboring municipalities or townships. No ZIP code is used on this site as a boundary, and the business address is stated in full on every page of this site.

What if my street is not on an area page?

Area pages cover documented districts and subdivisions, not every street in Belleville. Readers on other streets are invited to call (618) 677-6556 to discuss the work and the building. Office hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
